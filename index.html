Traitors

A social deduction game for 5 to 10 friends. Some of you are traitors. Nobody knows who.

Start a game Join a game

Free, no app, no sign-up. Each player uses their own phone.

How it works

  1. Start a party on Rocketcrab and share the code with your friends. Pick Traitors and start the game.
  2. Each player secretly learns their role. Most of you are Faithful. A few are Traitors, and they know each other.
  3. Each round, a captain picks a team for a mission, and everyone votes on the team in the open.
  4. The team decides the mission in secret. One sabotage is enough to fail it, and nobody learns who did it.
  5. The Faithful win by passing three missions. The Traitors win by sabotaging three, or if five teams in a row are rejected.

A traitor game to play with friends

Traitors is all about the conversation. The game keeps track of roles, teams and votes on everyone's phone, so nobody has to sit out as a moderator and nobody can peek at the cards. A game takes about 20 to 30 minutes.

  • In the same room

    Pass around accusations, not phones. Everyone votes on their own screen.

  • On a video call

    Talk on Zoom, Discord or FaceTime and play in a browser tab alongside it.

Inspired by The Resistance

If you have played The Resistance or Avalon, you already know how to play. Traitors uses the same team sizes and the same mission rules, so it is an easy way to play a game like The Resistance online for free. See how Traitors compares.

Questions

How many players do you need?

Traitors is for 5 to 10 players. Two to four of them are traitors, depending on the size of the group.

Do I need to download an app?

No. Everyone plays in their phone or computer's web browser through Rocketcrab. There is nothing to install and no account to create.

Can we play over a video call?

Yes. Talk on any video or voice call while each player uses their own device to vote. It also works with everyone in the same room.

Is this the game from the TV show The Traitors?

No. Traitors is an unofficial game based on the board game The Resistance. Like the show, a few hidden traitors try to fool the faithful, but the game is played through missions and votes rather than eliminations.

Is Traitors free?

Yes. Traitors is free to play, has no ads, and is open source.
