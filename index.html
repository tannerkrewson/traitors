Two ways to play

In both games, each player secretly learns their role. Most of you are Faithful. A few are Traitors, and they know each other.

Sabotage

Pass three missions before the traitors wreck them. A captain picks each team, everyone votes on it in the open, and the team decides the mission in secret.

Inspired by The Resistance. Nobody is eliminated.

Banishment

Vote out the traitors before they pick everyone off. Each day the round table banishes someone, and each night the traitors murder someone.

Inspired by Mafia, Werewolf and the TV show.

A traitor game to play with friends

Traitors is all about the conversation. The game keeps track of roles, teams and votes on everyone's phone, so nobody has to sit out as a moderator and nobody can peek at the cards. A game takes about 20 to 30 minutes.

  • In the same room

    Pass around accusations, not phones. Everyone votes on their own screen.

  • On a video call

    Talk on Zoom, Discord or FaceTime and play in a browser tab alongside it.

Inspired by classic party games

If you have played The Resistance or Avalon, you already know how to play Sabotage. It uses the same team sizes and mission rules, so it's an easy way to play a game like The Resistance online for free. See how Sabotage compares.

Banishment plays like Mafia or Werewolf, with no moderator needed and a few twists from the TV show: the traitors can recruit, and the last few players can vote to end the game. Read how Banishment works.

Questions

What's the difference between Sabotage and Banishment?

In Sabotage, teams go on missions and the traitors secretly sabotage them, like The Resistance. Nobody is eliminated. In Banishment, the group votes someone out each day and the traitors murder someone each night, like Mafia or Werewolf.

How many players do you need?

Traitors is made for 5 to 10 players, two to four of whom are traitors. With 3 or 4 players, bots take the empty seats so you can still play.

Do I need to download an app?

No. Everyone plays in their phone or computer's web browser through Rocketcrab. There is nothing to install and no account to create.

Can we play over a video call?

Yes. Talk on any video or voice call while each player uses their own device to vote. It also works with everyone in the same room.

Is this the game from the TV show The Traitors?

No. Traitors is an unofficial game based on the board game The Resistance. Like the show, a few hidden traitors try to fool the faithful, but the game is played through missions and votes rather than eliminations.

Is Traitors free?

Yes. Traitors is free to play, has no ads, and is open source.
