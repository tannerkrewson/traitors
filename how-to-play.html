How to play Traitors

Traitors is a game in which a team of faithful players is infiltrated by a group of traitors. The faithful must try to figure out who the traitors are, and the traitors must try to keep the faithful from figuring out their identity. The game is separated into five missions. The first team to "win" three missions wins the game.

In my opinion, the easiest way to learn how to play would be to watch someone else play, as it will help you get a feel for the dynamic of the game. Here's a video from Tabletop on YouTube of some people playing The Resistance, which Traitors is based on:

Players and roles

You need 5 to 10 players, each with their own phone or computer. When the game starts, each player can view their role in secret: either faithful or traitor. The faithful don't know who the traitors are, but the traitors are told who the other traitors are.

How it works

Here's a rundown of a game of Traitors:

  1. Start a party on Rocketcrab, and give the code to your friends to join. When everyone has joined in, pick Traitors and start the game.
  2. Each player views their role, then presses Begin.
  3. Next, a randomly selected captain will select a certain number of players to go on the mission.
    • If the captain is faithful, they should try to select players who they believe are not traitors to go on the mission.
    • The captain may also select themselves to go on the mission.
    • Everyone gets a turn as captain before anyone is captain again.
  4. Once the captain submits their selection, everyone will be prompted to vote on the captain's selection of players for the mission.
    • The faithful will want to approve mission teams of people who they believe to be faithful. They will want to keep traitors off the team, because it takes just one traitor to fail the mission altogether.
    • Be advised that game will show everyone each person's vote on the results page.
  5. The vote results are then displayed based on majority vote.
    • If the majority votes to reject the team, a new captain will be selected and the process will start over again.
    • If the vote is a tie, the selection will be rejected.
    • If five teams in a row are rejected, the traitors win the game.
    • If the selection is approved, the mission commences.
  6. During the mission, the players selected to go on the mission will get to choose to either pass or sabotage the mission.
    • If one or more players sabotage the mission, it will fail.
    • Again, everyone on the mission must vote to pass it for it to pass, with one exception: if the game has seven or more players and it is the fourth mission, two or more players must sabotage the mission for it to fail.
    • If you are faithful, it will always be in your best interest to pass the mission. Although the game will allow it, do not sabotage the mission as a faithful player, because it just hurts your team.
    • If you are a traitor, although it will sometimes be in your best interest to sabotage the mission, other times you may want to pass the mission to throw the other players off your scent.
  7. The results of the mission will then be displayed on screen.
    • Unlike the results screen of the player vote, the mission results screen does not show how players voted.
    • It does, however, show how many pass and sabotage votes there were.
    • For example, if a mission has two players on it, and one player votes pass, and the other votes sabotage, the other players will now know that one of the two of you is a traitor.
  8. The game will now continue to the next mission, starting over the process of picking a captain and having them select players.
  9. Once either team, faithful or traitors, has gotten 3 missions, they win the game. To clarify:
    • Once 3 missions are passed, the faithful win.
    • Once 3 missions are sabotaged, the traitors win.
  10. When a team is declared winner, the traitors that game are displayed on the screen for public scrutiny.

Tips

  • Watch the team votes. A traitor who approves a team with another traitor on it may be protecting a friend.
  • Keep track of who was on each sabotaged mission. The same names showing up twice is suspicious.
  • Don't worry if someone disconnects in the middle of the game. Just have someone (doesn't have to be the same person) join the party. They will be able to take the disconnected player's seat, and the game will continue.

Traitors (originally called Snakeout) was programmed by myself, Tanner Krewson, with React, and runs on Rocketcrab.
