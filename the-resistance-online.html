Play a game like The Resistance online

The Resistance, designed by Don Eskridge, is one of the best hidden role games ever made. If your group can't get around the same table, Traitors is a free way to play the same kind of game online. Everyone joins from their own phone or computer, and the game handles the roles, team votes and secret mission cards for you.

Traitors is an unofficial fan game. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by Indie Boards & Cards. If you enjoy it, the physical game is well worth buying.

What's the same

  • 5 to 10 players, with the same number of hidden spies (called traitors here) for each group size.
  • The same team size for each of the five missions.
  • Team votes are public, and a tie rejects the team.
  • Mission cards are secret. One sabotage fails a mission, except mission 4 with 7 or more players, which needs two.
  • The first side to win three missions wins, and the spies win if five teams in a row are rejected.

What's different

The Resistance Traitors
Teams Resistance and spies Faithful and traitors
Who picks the team The leader, passed clockwise A captain, chosen at random from players who haven't had a turn yet
Setup Shuffle and deal cards, close eyes Automatic, on everyone's phone
Where Around a table Same room or any video call
Expansions Plot cards, Avalon roles and more None, just the core game
Fewer than 5 players Not supported 3 or 4 players, with bots in the empty seats
Price A boxed game Free

How to start

  1. One person starts a party and shares the code.
  2. Everyone else opens rocketcrab.com/join on their phone and enters the code.
  3. Start the game, check your role in secret, and play.
